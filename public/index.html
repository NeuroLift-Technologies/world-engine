Training Loop

Perceive → decide → act → learn → repeat

The World Engine owns the physical simulation layer. Each tick of the loop is authoritative and reproducible.

01 · PERCEIVE

Agents observe the world through sensory channels: sight, hearing, objects, NPCs, and their own internal state.

02 · DECIDE

Intent is formed by downstream models. The engine evaluates options against needs, goals, and social context.

03 · ACT

Decisions become movement and manipulation. Articulated bodies animate. World state mutates deterministically.

04 · LEARN

Outcomes flow back to the training layer, which fuses trait behaviour into Avatar, Aide, and Advocate intelligence.

Avatar · Aide · Advocate

Three roles, one resident.

Each inhabitant can express itself as any of these roles — and transition between them as scenarios change.

A
ADHD trait modelling

Avatar

Lives the experience directly. Attention, impulse, and executive-function traits are modelled as embodied behaviour inside the world.

Ai
Coaching expertise

Aide

Provides guidance and scaffolding. Understands the Avatar's state, offers strategies, and adjusts support over time.

Ad
Personalized 1:20

Advocate

The fused result. Avatar and Aide integrated into a single personalized advocate delivered to the end user.

Architecture

Where this repo stops — and what it hands off.

NeuroLift Technologies Simulation Environment is the deterministic runtime where AI Avatars (with ADHD traits) and AI Aides live. This repo owns the physical simulation layer and is the authoritative simulation training environment for the Avatar-Aide-Advocate system.
